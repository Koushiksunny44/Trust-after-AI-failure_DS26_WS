# Sources and scope

## Dataset

Ouyang, L., Jahng, J., & Woo, S. (2026). The Transparency Paradox: Decoupling Perceived and Behavioral Trust through Trust Calibration in Human-AI Interaction. Mendeley Data, Version 1. DOI: https://doi.org/10.17632/5w56cp2ftr.1 . Dataset licence: CC BY 4.0, as stated on its official page https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/5w56cp2ftr/1 . The bundle contains the unchanged CSV supplied for this project. It includes a free-text feedback field, which is excluded from numerical analysis and not reproduced in the notebook's first/last-record display. Public availability does not establish the original consent conditions or UE's secondary-use determination.

The independent WoA derivatives and analysis tables are this project's analytical modifications. They are not described as author-corrected source data. Original attribution must be retained in future reuse. The source CSV SHA256 is recorded in the executed manifest.

## Anchor paper

Graf, B. (2025). Sounds good, doesn't work – an experimental study on the dynamic development of trust in digital systems under inconsistent information about developer reputation and system errors. Computers in Human Behavior, 172, 108736. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2025.108736 . Full uploaded paper inspected, especially pp. 4–7 and 10–11.
